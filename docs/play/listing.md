## Full description

Beer Debt turns beer into miles you owe.

Tap + Beer when you have one. That's a mile on your tab. Go for a run and Health Connect pays it off automatically, oldest beer first. Ignore it for a day and it starts to grow.

Run more than you owe and you bank a few beers for later. Banked beers slowly expire, so drink them or lose them.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap + Beer. One tap, that's it.
• Run. Your running workouts come straight from Health Connect, from whatever app or watch you already use. Only runs count, so walking to the bar doesn't pay for the beer.
• Watch the tab shrink. Or grow, if you skip the run.
• Run every day. Two days in a row and interest on your tab pauses. Miss a day and it starts again.

MAKE YOUR OWN RULES
• How many miles a beer costs
• How fast an unpaid beer grows, and how long you get before it starts
• How many beers you can bank

KEEPS YOU HONEST
• You can't type in miles. Only real runs count.
• Forgot one? Date a beer back. Tapped by mistake? Remove it.
• A home screen widget and an optional weekly recap so the tab is hard to ignore.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. Nothing leaves your phone. Beer Debt only reads your running workouts from Health Connect and never writes anything back.

Drink now. Run later.
